# Glossary

Terms used in this repo. Add new or renamed terms here as they come up.

- **Behavior**: a reusable program attached to a unit or group that decides its actions each tick (movement, targeting, when to fire). Packaged behaviors ship with the game; custom behaviors are player-authored.
- **Cooldown**: the delay after a weapon fires before it can fire again.
- **Focus fire**: several units attacking the same target, picked by a rule.
- **Kite / kiting**: attacking an enemy while staying out of its reach, usually by retreating between shots.
- **Macro**: big-picture play: economy, production, build order, army composition.
- **Micro**: fine control of individual units in combat.
- **Stutter-step / flicker-shot**: moving between shots and stopping only for the instant the weapon is ready, so you lose as little movement as possible.
